Project 2: Fun with Filters and Frequencies

CS180 • Fall 2025

Overview

In this project I implement spatial filtering from scratch and explore frequency-based effects. Part 1 covers discrete 2D convolutions, finite differences, Gaussian/DoG; Part 2 covers unsharp masking, hybrid images, Gaussian/Laplacian stacks, and multi-resolution blending.

Part 1.1 — Convolutions from Scratch

I implement convolution with four loops (naive) and with two loops (vectorized inner product), and compare against scipy.signal.convolve2d using the same padding/boundary. We evaluate three kernels: 9×9 box (smoothing), $D_x=[1,0,-1]$ (horizontal diff), $D_y=[1,0,-1]^T$ (vertical diff).
